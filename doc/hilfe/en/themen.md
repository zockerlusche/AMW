# Topics: what applies now

A topic collects all decisions about one system – “saving” or “map look”, for example – and has a maintained summary “applies now”. Nobody has to read old decisions one by one.

## You need
- A maintainer: the agent who writes the summary and keeps it up to date.

## Steps
1. **Create a topic** `amw thema neu "<name>" --pfleger <agent>`. You, the lead and the project's co-developer may create topics; you or the lead set the maintainer and the project style.
2. **Write the summary** The maintainer writes it with `amw thema pflegen <topic> --text-datei <file>`.
3. **Keep it current** When a new decision about the topic is added, the summary counts as outdated. The maintainer updates it or confirms it with `amw thema bestaetigen <topic>`.

## Common problems
- **“outdated since B…”** – A newer decision is not in the summary yet. The maintainer is reminded.
- **“No summary yet”** – Step 2 is missing.

## Next
- One topic can be the project style (`amw thema einstellen <topic> --stil`); all agents then see it in their start information.
- **Menu item “Topics”:** every topic shows exactly one status: “waiting for you” (a new version is there), “check again” (a new decision came after your approval – “because of: B…” is shown; the AI checks first, so it is not under “For you”), “in progress” or “approved”. Open a topic and you read its text as a whole. On the PC you only get what is possible right now: **Approve**, **Send back** (with a note – it goes to the maintainer as a task), **Edit** (your text becomes the new version and counts as approved by you; the maintainer gets a note) and **Mark for deletion** (nothing is deleted; you can unmark at any time). With “+ Topic” you create a topic yourself (title and text) – it is then “in progress” for the AI. On the phone you read along. Topics waiting for you appear under “For you” on the overview and lead straight there. Nobody sets the status by hand – AMW derives it from versions, decisions and your actions. Agents see it with `amw thema status`.
- **Cross-reading:** When the maintainer delivers a new version, a fresh agent reads it first – it only reads, changes nothing and costs a few cents. Meanwhile the topic shows “being cross-read · since …” and is not with you yet. Its objections go to the maintainer, who delivers a new version or leaves it as it is. Then the topic waits for you, and below the status you can expand “Cross-read: version … · model · cost” with the objections. One cross-read per round – the maintainer's answer to the objections comes to you without a second cross-read. To switch it off or use the larger model: `amw projekt einstellen themen_gegenlesen=aus` (or `=sonnet`, default `haiku`) – you or the lead set this.
- **Tasks that refer to a topic:** `amw aufgabe aendern <no> --thema TH3,TH5` (remove with `--ohne-thema TH3`) – the lead, you, the co-developer or the task's agent set this. When an approved topic gets a new version – from the maintainer or from you –, AMW reports the open tasks that refer to it as “Thema geändert – prüfen” (topic changed – check) to their agent, one message per agent. p packages go to the lead; a running one keeps running. The task status does not change. `amw aufgabe zeige <no>` lists the topics.
- **Where the project stands:** The top of the page shows “Concept · 2 of 7 topics approved” (topics marked for deletion do not count). There is no button to close it: when you approve the last open topic, the page first says “This closes the Concept gate” – it only counts once you confirm. Then it shows “Rough planning · Concept done: 7 of 7 topics approved”. The same question comes when your own text or “Mark for deletion” settles the last open topic. If you then send a topic back, a new one is added, the maintainer delivers a new version or one is on “check again”, the concept is open again – AMW derives this. It records when it closed and with which versions. Agents see the line at the top of `amw thema status`.
- **Gaps between topics and packages:** No package without a topic, no approved topic without a package. AMW shows both gaps itself: Planning shows “Gaps: 1 approved topic without a package · 3 packages without a topic” at the top, with the list when expanded. The lead is told about every new gap once. To close a gap: link the package to the topic (`amw aufgabe neu … --thema TH3` or `amw aufgabe aendern <no> --thema TH3`), note “nothing to build” on the topic (`amw thema vermerk TH3 --nichts-zu-bauen --grund …`, lead, you or the co-developer) or – for internal upkeep such as clean-up – give a reason when creating the task (`--ohne-thema-grund …`). With `amw projekt einstellen thema_pflicht=an`, `amw aufgabe neu` stops without a topic and without a reason; otherwise you only get a note. `amw thema luecken` shows the list in the terminal.
- **Close the concept and rough planning:** Once all topics are approved, you close the concept (`amw thema konzept-abschliessen`). A fresh agent (read-only, Opus) then plans the whole concept once: it looks for duplicates, topics that are too big, unclear points, contradictions and topics without a milestone, and cuts milestones – each with a goal (referring to the topics), “done when”, a demo or report at the end, order, rough effort and cost. Its questions go to the lead. Every run is a version; all stay visible (`amw thema grobplan`, plan again with `--neu`). With your yes (`amw thema grobplan-ja`) AMW creates the milestones and approves them roughly. Who says yes works as for topics (with co+lead: co-developer and lead one yes each).
- **Notes for the planner:** If something in a draft does not fit, give the planner a note on a card (`amw thema hinweis grob --zu M2 --text …`, for a topic's plan `TH3` instead of `grob`) and let it revise (`amw thema ueberarbeiten grob`). It presents a new version; every version stays visible, with new, changed and dropped items marked.
- **Change to a planned topic:** When you approve a new version of a topic that already has packages, the planner proposes only the change: new packages, and changed or no longer needed packages that have not started – plus rework only for finished packages the change really affects (with a reason); finished packages are not rework by default. You approve this delta as one block (`amw thema plan-ja TH3`), and that starts it: with your yes a new milestone is approved, rough and detailed, and runs; if the plan of an existing one was approved, it stays approved. If the change creates a new milestone, only those who also approve rough plans can approve it. Running packages are finished. If the plan for the change does not fit, the planner corrects it once by itself; if it still does not fit, “Project path” shows the reason in the step Detailed planning, and with a note you have it revised.
- **Planning from topics:** In a new project (no milestones yet) the rough planning after closing the concept comes first; after that, and in projects with milestones: when you approve a topic, a fresh agent (read-only, Sonnet) drafts milestones and packages from it – as data, not as text. The lead sees the draft and says yes or changes it; only then does AMW create everything and link it to the topic. If a topic needs nothing, it says “nothing to build” with a reason. Small packages (S, and M of type mechanical, no user interface) are planned as p with an acceptance command and narrow areas; interactive only with a reason, otherwise the yes reports “Achtung” (see p packages). By hand: `amw thema planen TH3` (lead or co-developer), view with `amw thema plan TH3`, accept with `amw thema plan-ja TH3`. Open drafts also show at the top of Planning under “Milestones” – with “Yes – create” and “Plan again”. To switch off: `amw projekt einstellen themen_planer=aus`. Cost: at most $5 per project and day for all planner runs (`planer_tagesgrenze_usd`, 0 = no limit); once the limit is reached, AMW plans the remaining topics the next day and tells the lead.
- **Importing a concept (for the lead):** If a project's concept exists as Markdown, it becomes topics semi-automatically. 1. Put a copy of the file in the project folder (the original stays as it is). 2. Preview: `amw thema uebernehmen --datei <copy.md>` – shows which headings become topics (the level below the title, otherwise `--ebene 2`), what is not assigned (title, text before the first section, duplicate or existing names) and the count check. Nothing is written. 3. Read the count check: lines with text, headings, identifiers (A12, TH3, §1.5 …) and links in the original must appear exactly as often in the topics plus the unassigned parts – then it says “bestanden” (passed). Otherwise it names what is missing; adjust the headings in the copy and run it again. 4. Create: the same command with `--anlegen`. Each heading becomes a topic, the section below it its first version. Nothing is approved: the drafts wait for the human, no reviewer or planner starts. The original is attached as a document to every new topic, the report with the count check is a document of its own. 5. Check the drafts: `amw thema status`, set a maintainer (`amw thema einstellen TH… --pfleger …`), rework the texts. Only files in the project's own folder and only topics of that project; allowed for the lead, the human and the co-developer.
