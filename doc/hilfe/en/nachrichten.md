# Messages: inbox and sent

Everything you write to agents and everything they write to you is in one place – for the project you picked at the top; to see another project, pick it at the top. Notices from the Workbench itself show “System” as the sender. For each recipient you see how far a message has got – and as long as nobody has read it, you can withdraw it.

## You need
- The Workbench at the PC or the online site. Menu › Messages.

## Steps
1. **Write** Messages › “+ Write” – pick a recipient (one agent or everyone), type, text. From an agent (“Message”) the recipient is already filled in.
2. **Write from anywhere** Wherever you look at something – topic, milestone, decision, run, review – there is “✉ Message about this”: the reference is filled in and a recipient suggested (you can change it). Topics and milestones show “Messages about this” below as a small conversation. For tasks and documents you keep writing in their own field – that carries the reference too.
3. **Follow the status** “Sent” tab: per recipient sent › notified › read › done. Hover over a step to see the time.
4. **Read replies** Clicking a message shows the conversation on the right – your message and what came back. On a phone it opens as its own view (“‹ Back to list” returns).
5. **Work through the inbox** “Inbox” tab: unread is bold. “Reply” writes back, the reference comes along; “Done” removes it from the count in the menu.
6. **Withdraw** Sent › pick a message › “Withdraw” – as long as **no** recipient has read or done it. It disappears for every recipient and stays with you as “withdrawn”.

## Common problems
- **A message stays at “sent” for a long time** – The agent is not running, or it is a chat lead that only reads on its own rhythm. The start page shows whether it is running.
- **“Withdraw” is greyed out** – A recipient has already read it. Send a new message (“please ignore”).
- **The reference is missing** – Messages via “+ Write” in the menu are general; write via “✉ Message about this” on the item and it comes along.

## Next
- The agents' questions to you are in the “Questions” tab; your answer then shows in Sent, with the question as reference.
- The start page shows only a card with the newest messages for you – one click takes you to the inbox.
- On the PC the list and the message scroll separately, as in a mail program: you can read on the right without losing the list.

## Questions
### What do the steps mean?
**sent** – the message is in AMW. **notified** – the service has woken the agent (chat leads read on their next round). **read** – the agent has fetched it with `amw lesen`. **done** – it has dealt with it. **being sent …** – online only, until the PC has taken it over.

### How does a reply end up in the right conversation?
When an agent writes only to you, its reply attaches itself to your last unanswered message to it (up to 2 days old) and inherits its reference.
